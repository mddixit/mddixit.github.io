About me

I'm a research-track Master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, working in the Biohybrid and Organic Robotics Group with Dr. Victoria Webster-Wood.

I like working at the point where hardware meets intelligence: molding silicone actuators, laying out custom PCBs, writing closed-loop controllers, and training neural networks so a soft robot can figure out how to move.

Before CMU, I earned my B.S. at Purdue, did soft-materials research in the Chortos Lab, led a senior design team building an underwater manta ray, and spent six months as a design engineering co-op at Cummins.
